## 1. What is Darkhorse?

Darkhorse is a launchpad and swap for assets on **RGB**, a protocol that issues tokens on Bitcoin itself. It runs two separate free campaigns, both tied to your Bitcoin wallet:

| Campaign | You earn | How | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Genesis + Mining** | **Horseshoes** (points) | tasks, then mining every ~12h | ongoing |
| **$RGB Mystery Box** | **$RGB** tokens + 1 OG NFT (+ a chance at a whitelist) | open one box per wallet | **~14 Nov 2026** (watch the countdown) |

Why Horseshoes matter: Darkhorse plans to give **up to 6% of its future token supply** to Horseshoe holders, split by each person's share of all Horseshoes.

## 2. Install the Darkhorse Wallet

You need this for the $RGB box and to receive RGB assets. **Desktop only:** phones can't install browser extensions.

1. On a computer, open **Chrome** (or Brave / Edge) and go to the [Darkhorse Wallet page on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/darkhorse-wallet/fegliconnjmegcbkelmcbmhniphafiem).
   - Check the publisher and that the link matches the one on dhorse.fun. Fake wallets copy names.
2. Click **Add to Chrome**, then pin it (puzzle icon, then pin).
3. Open it and choose **Create new wallet**.
4. **Write the recovery phrase on paper, in order.** It's the only way to restore your wallet and claim rewards. Never type it into a website, never screenshot it, never share it.
5. Set a password (it only locks the wallet on this computer).
6. Copy your **Bitcoin address** (starts with `bc1p…`). This address is your identity: **all your $RGB and rewards are recorded to it.**

> Already use **OKX** or **UniSat**? They work for signing in on some pages, but the **$RGB box must be opened with the Darkhorse Wallet**.

## 4. Mining Horseshoes

Free mining that rewards Bitcoin holders, and **past** holders too: if you ever held inscriptions, Runes or BRC-20, you still count even if you sold them.

### Step by step
1. Finish the Genesis tasks (section 3).
2. Open the **mining page**: https://genesis.dhorse.fun/mine
3. **Link your Bitcoin wallet(s)**: up to **5 addresses**, each verified by a signature. A signature costs nothing and sends no transaction. Use OKX / UniSat / Xverse or your Darkhorse Wallet.
4. Click **Activate mining power**. Rewards only start from activation.
5. **Come back and press Claim before each round ends** (see the warning below).

### How mining power is calculated
| Source | Power | Max |
|---|---|---|
| BTC balance | +30 per full 0.0001 BTC | 3,000 |
| Each distinct inscription collection / Rune / BRC-20 ever held | +30 each (up to 30 assets) | 900 |
| Bonus assets*: ORDI, SATS, RATS, NodeMonkes, Bitcoin Puppets, Runestone, Quantum Cats, Bitcoin Frogs, Taproot Wizards, OMB, DOG•GO•TO•THE•MOON, MAGIC•INTERNET•MONEY, PUPS, UNCOMMON•GOODS | +200 each, stacks | 2,800 |
| **Total from assets** | | **6,700** |

*Bonus assets also get the normal +30. Example: holding DOG = 30 + 200 = **230 power**.

**Status boosts** (no assets? you still mine):
| Status | Minimum power | Boost |
|---|---|---|
| Knight | 100 | +10% |
| Genesis Partner | 100 | +30% |
| Both | 100 | +40% |

**Output:** ~**0.224 Horseshoes per day per point of power**. A Knight with no assets mines ~25/day; 1,000 power mines ~224/day.

### ⚠️ Claim every round or lose it
- A **round = 72 Bitcoin blocks ≈ 12 hours**, and it's the same for everyone.
- Rewards are only yours after you click **Claim** (minimum 1 Horseshoe; you can claim several times per round).
- **Unclaimed Horseshoes are burned at the end of each round.** Set a reminder twice a day.

### Rules worth knowing
- Each address can belong to **one** account. Link only addresses **you control**: future token redemption needs that wallet's signature.
- You get **one** linked-address change, and it **burns 200 Horseshoes**.
- Each address's history is scanned **once**. Changes apply from the **next round**.
- Self-referrals or shuffling assets between accounts can get you **suspended**.

## 5. The $RGB Mystery Box

**210,000,000 $RGB**, free, first come first served, **one box per Darkhorse Wallet**, each box with **1 OG NFT**.

### Step 1: Build your Power (you need more than 0 to open a box)
**Power = X Power + BTC Power + LNFi Bonus.** More Power means more $RGB, and **1,000+ Power** enters you into the **Free Mint Whitelist** draw.

| Power | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **X Power** (up to 5,000) | Connect X | Blue check **or** 200+ followers required. Read-only, never posts or follows. |
| **BTC Power** | Verify a BTC address by signature | Any wallet (OKX / UniSat / Xverse / OneKey). No transfer. Snapshot 30 Oct 2026. |
| **LNFi Bonus** | Automatic, if you traded PPRGB, SOON, CATO, MOULA or SUS on LNFi | Snapshot 30 Oct 2026 |

All three are optional, but you need at least one.

### Step 2: Open the box
1. Open **https://rgb.dhorse.fun/?ref=DHORSE-SBAH6S&lang=en** (referral is set at first sign-in).
2. Do steps ① to ③ above for Power, then **④ Sign in with Darkhorse Wallet**. It's a signature only, no transaction.
3. **⑤ Mint $RGB & open your box.** The amount is fixed and recorded the moment it opens.
4. Whitelist draws happen automatically: Free Mint first, then Lv2. These are UNSEEN NFT whitelists, at most one per box. Winners are sent to UNSEEN every 1 to 2 days.

> One Darkhorse Wallet can hold up to **5 addresses** (holdings merged). Each address and X account belongs to one wallet and **locks when the box opens**.

### Deadline
The campaign runs **31 Oct to 14 Nov 2026** (watch the countdown on the page). After that, **boxes close, unclaimed referral rewards expire, and unopened $RGB is burned.**

## 8. Stay safe

- **Your recovery phrase is your money.** Darkhorse never asks for it. Only ever type it into the Darkhorse Wallet extension itself.
- **Every step here uses signatures, never payments.** If anything asks you to send BTC, approve spending, or "pay a fee to claim", stop: it's a scam.
- **Install the wallet only** from the Chrome Web Store link on **dhorse.fun**.
- **Expect fake airdrop sites and DMs.** Official links come from [@RGBHorse](https://x.com/RGBHorse) and [dhorse.fun](https://dhorse.fun) only.
- **Link only wallets you control.** You'll need to sign with them to redeem later.

## 9. FAQ

**Can I do this on my phone?** Genesis tasks and mining claims: yes, in a mobile browser with a mobile BTC wallet. The **$RGB box needs the desktop Darkhorse Wallet**.

**Do I need BTC?** No. Knights mine with a minimum of 100 power without any assets, and the box only needs some Power (e.g. from X). Holding BTC or Bitcoin assets just boosts you.

**I forgot to claim mining rewards.** Unclaimed Horseshoes are burned each round and can't be recovered. Claim twice a day.

**When do I get $RGB?** It's airdropped in batches to your Darkhorse Wallet address after the box campaign ends.

**What are Horseshoes worth?** Nothing yet. They're non-transferable points. Darkhorse plans a share of up to 6% of its future token for holders, but makes no promises.

**Is $RGB the same as Bitlight's RGB token?** No. This $RGB is Darkhorse's free RGB v0.11 faucet asset, unrelated to the RGB Association or Bitlight.
